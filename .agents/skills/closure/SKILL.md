# Closure UX Law

Use for carousels, horizontal lists, galleries, and pagers. Show a deliberate portion of the next item only when more content is genuinely available. Keep the peek visually intentional and avoid cropping critical content. Support swipe, pointer, keyboard, and accessible labels. Do not rely on a partial card as the only discovery cue. Validate responsive behavior and discoverability.
