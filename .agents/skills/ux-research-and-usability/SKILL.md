# UX Research and Usability

Use this skill when a design decision needs user evidence or when evaluating whether a flow is understandable.

## Plan
- Define the decision or uncertainty being investigated.
- Identify target users and representative tasks.
- Write neutral task prompts that do not reveal the expected solution.
- Decide what to observe: completion, errors, hesitation, navigation, confidence, and recovery.
- Collect only necessary information and obtain appropriate consent.

## Analyze
- Separate observed behavior from interpretation.
- Record task context and severity.
- Look for repeated patterns, not isolated preference statements.
- Distinguish usability problems from feature requests.
- Avoid overgeneralizing from small or biased samples.

## Deliver
Research question, method, participant/context limitations, task findings, evidence, severity, recommendations, and follow-up validation.

## Guardrails
Never fabricate participants, quotes, analytics, or test results. Do not collect sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and properly handled.
