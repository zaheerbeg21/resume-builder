# Figure-Ground UX Law

Use for dialogs, sheets, menus, and focused task flows. Use a scrim and clear foreground surface when focus is needed. Preserve contrast, context, clear actions, and accessible focus management. Do not over-dim, block background interaction inconsistently, or use modals for trivial notices. Validate dismissal, focus, screen-reader announcement, and contrast.
