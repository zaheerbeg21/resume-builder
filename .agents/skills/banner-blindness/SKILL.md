# Banner Blindness UX Law

Use for notices, recommendations, upgrade prompts, and announcements. Place task-critical information near the decision it affects. Use contextual wording and familiar product styling. Clearly label sponsored or promotional content. Never disguise ads as system alerts, use fake controls, or manufacture urgency. Validate relevance, comprehension, and dismissal behavior.
